About

From the field to the dashboard

I'm a Data Analyst based in Kumasi, working on agricultural traceability, sustainability reporting, and GIS-based supply chain systems for cocoa and cashew commodities. Most of my work sits between two worlds: messy field data collected through SurveyCTO and mobile surveys, and the clean, queryable pipelines and dashboards that operations and sustainability teams actually use.

I'm fluent in Twi as well as English, which has been useful for field-facing work and farmer engagement, alongside a background in rural community training. Outside of my day-to-day role I've been building small, focused web projects — from anomaly-detection tooling for survey data to straightforward front-end sites like this one.
